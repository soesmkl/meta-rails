## Purpose

The webpage helps users identify construction and repair materials, find a suitable match for their project, and discover where to buy it.

## How users search

- **Photo search** — Users can upload a photo of a material they want to identify or replace.
- **Text search** — Users can describe the material or the job in their own words, even if they do not know the product name.
- **Combined search** — Users can upload a photo and add details such as dimensions, intended use, or required finish to narrow down the match.

## What users receive

- **Material matches** — The webpage suggests likely materials based on the photo, the description, or both.
- **Matching details** — Each suggestion explains which features match and highlights any details the user should confirm before buying.
- **Where to buy** — The webpage suggests relevant shops or suppliers where users can check price and availability.
